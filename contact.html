What the thirty minutes covers

In this order.

  1. The duty as it applies to your firm, and the dates already fixed against it.
  2. How the work is being done at your firm today, in your own words.
  3. The five screens, opened live, against a date you choose.
  4. What the pack looks like when somebody asks for that date range.
  5. What we would need from you, and what we would never ask for.

We do not ask for customer data to run a walkthrough, and we will not take a copy of any of it. If a deeper session is useful after the first call, it runs under a written agreement.

Who this is for

If you hold money that belongs to somebody else, this is your duty and not your bank's.

The duty attaches to the firm holding the money. No bank, processor or sponsor carries it for you.
