A controls question

Two different tests, and only one of them is suitability.

In investments the test is why this investment suited the objectives and risk tolerance of the client. Across 105 firms, suitability determinations were recorded without the basis for them. Client money is a different question entirely: not whether the advice was right, but whether the controls happened and the money stayed where it belonged.

Retail Payment Activities Act

On the independent review, stated precisely. Every provider holding end user funds owes an independent review at least once every three years. The reviewer may be internal to the firm, so nobody has to be hired. Registration date plus three years is the deadline for that firm, it is per firm and rolling, and no market-wide first review date has been published.

4orm does not do the independent review. 4orm produces the material the review runs on. The regulations disqualify anyone who helped establish or maintain the framework from performing the review, and this platform does exactly that work. The reviewer writes and signs the findings.

Why one platform covers four markets

The duty is the same six steps everywhere it appears.

This is the reason a firm in one market and a firm in another buy the same thing. The vocabulary is local. The obligation underneath is not.

01

Establish the need

What the person is trying to achieve, in their words, before a product is named.

02

Consider the options

What was available, what was weighed, and what was set aside.

03

Recommend with a reason

The reason recorded while it is fresh, not reconstructed afterwards.

04

Disclose what matters

Material risks, conflicts and compensation, in terms the person can act on.

05

Obtain informed consent

An acknowledgement that is dated, attributable and specific to what was disclosed.

06

Keep a re-performable record

A file another qualified person could pick up and reach the same conclusion from.

Real estate carries the same shape and is the fifth market by the same logic. Complaints opened there rose 88 per cent in a single year, to 2,878 in 2025, and brokerages in Ontario file annually. It is not on this page because it is not where the first paid work is.
